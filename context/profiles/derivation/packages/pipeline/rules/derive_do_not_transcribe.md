# Derive, Do Not Transcribe

Source material becomes context by transformation, not by copying: each
derived artifact speaks in the consuming module's own vocabulary,
compressed and agent-oriented, citing the source rather than restaging
its prose.

## Rationale

Transcription duplicates content that drifts from its source and imports
the source's framing, audience, and vocabulary without adaptation. A
derived rule earns its place by being shorter, sharper, and aimed at the
agent's behavior; a derived reference earns its place by being dense and
self-contained where the source is exploratory.

When the exact wording matters — a definition, a quotable line — cite
it with a locator so the reader can check it at the site, rather than
re-typing it into the derived file.
