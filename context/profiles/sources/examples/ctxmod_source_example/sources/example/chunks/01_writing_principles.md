Write to inform, not persuade. A reader takes the same process every
run; the writing levers make that process predictable. State claims
directly and affirmatively — negation-for-drama ("it's not X, it's Y")
is replaced by direct affirmatives, while steering-level negation
(banning a behavior drags it into attention) is replaced by positive
targets. Lead with the words the reader acts on, and let every claim
about a produced artifact answer "how do you know?" with real generated
output when a real instance exists, and with a marked sketch when it
does not.
