# Summaries and Analyses

`summary/`, `analysis/`, and `overview/` files are scanned Markdown —
ordinary context documents that happen to live inside a source, citable
in their own right through the source's citation key and a `#`-specifier
or declared locator.

## Summary versus analysis

- **Summary** maps a part of *its own* source: what a document says,
  compressed and located. A summary may cite its own source and others,
  but its center of gravity is faithful mapping, not argument.
- **Analysis** integrates material across passages or across sources:
  comparison, tension, synthesis. Cross-source citation is normal here.

Both follow the ordinary writing principles: direct claims, pointers
over pasting, and locators at the passages that carry the material.

## SOURCE.md

`SOURCE.md` at the source root is the source's entry document: what the
source is, what it contains, how its directories are organized, and
where a reader should start. It is scanned like any body.

## Choosing what to summarize

Map the principal documents, not every document: a summary earns its
place when module content will cite the material it covers. An unsummarized
document is reachable through its chunks; a principal document nobody can
cite is a gap.
