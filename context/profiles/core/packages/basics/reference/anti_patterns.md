# Writing Anti-Patterns

Patterns that degrade agent-facing documentation. Eliminate
systematically; the revision checklist at the end is the standing pass.

## Negation-for-drama

The most persistent tell: creating false depth through "it's not X,
it's Y". Replace with the direct affirmative: "Trust requires genuine
compliance", not "It's not about compliance. It's about trust."

## Formulaic expressions

| Class | Reject | Prefer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Corporate jargon | leverage, streamline, synergy, game-changer, paradigm shift | use, simplify, cooperation, significant advancement |
| Epistemic verbs | delve, underscore, illuminate, facilitate, bolster | examine, show, clarify, enable, strengthen |
| Evaluative adjectives | crucial, paramount, invaluable, transformative | important, primary, essential, or be specific |
| Metaphorical nouns | tapestry, landscape, symphony, realm | network, field, integration, area |

## Filler phrases

Delete entirely: "In the ever-evolving landscape of X…", "It is crucial
to recognize that…", "What sets X apart is…", "At its core, X
represents…". Replace with the direct statement or nothing.

## Structural predictability

Reject lockstep three-part structures, always-first topic sentences, and
uniform paragraph lengths. Vary paragraph lengths (two to eight
sentences) and let structure reflect content complexity. Reject the
participial formula ("The system analyzes data, revealing insights") and
semicolon overconnection.

## Transition dependency and burstiness

Vary formal connectors (furthermore, moreover, therefore, nonetheless
each at most once per section, with plain alternatives). Match the
natural length mix: mostly medium sentences with short and long
interleaved, varied openings, and measured emotional calibration —
claims sized to evidence.

## Revision checklist

- No "not just X, but Y" or "it's not X, it's Y" constructions.
- No jargon, epistemic-verb, or metaphorical-noun vocabulary above.
- No filler phrases; delete rather than rephrase.
- Mixed sentence lengths, openings, and structures.
- Claims proportional to evidence; no invented values — unknowns are
  marked as unknown, never filled in.
