# The Two Loads

Every document and pointer spends one of two budgets.

**Context load** is the cost of always-loaded material on the agent's
window: a profile line, a rule, a skill summary — anything resident
every turn pays budget and attention whether or not it fires.

**Cognitive load** is the cost on the human of knowing what exists and
when to reach for it. The human is the index. Spend it where human
judgement matters; remove it where it does not.

Material behind a pointer escapes context load at the price of the
pointer's own line. Material with no pointer rides entirely on cognitive
load. Classify before you write: profile and rule content is context
load; the reference tree behind links is disclosed material; knowing
which directory holds what is cognitive load.

The two loads set the price of every placement decision in the
[information hierarchy](information_hierarchy.md): the hierarchy decides
where material goes; the loads decide what the placement costs.
